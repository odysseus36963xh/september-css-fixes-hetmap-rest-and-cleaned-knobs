⚡ Auto Vocab Builder

Paste your target-language text below (a chapter, a whole book, a gospel presentation, whatever). This finds the top most-frequent single words, then the top most-frequent 2-word combos, then the top most-frequent 3-word combos — and stacks all three lists into Column A, ready for you to add translations in Column B.








Tableizer

Paste a large block of text below. Each sentence gets placed on its own row, and every word and punctuation symbol (commas, periods, colons, parentheses, etc.) gets its own cell. A blank line in your text (like the gap between paragraphs in a book) becomes a blank row in the sheet, to keep things clean.





Vocab Highlighter

Paste (or type) vocabulary the student already knows — one word per line, or separated by commas. Hit Highlight and every cell anywhere on the sheet that matches one of those words exactly turns yellow. Whatever's left un-highlighted is what the student hasn't learned yet, so they can focus their attention there instead of re-reading words they already know.


Once you've highlighted the words already known, harvest everything else: this pulls every un-highlighted word/symbol out of the sheet, row by row, keeping the original sentence order — highlighted (known) cells are simply skipped, so a sentence may come out a little broken if it had known words in the middle of it. Paste the result into Frequency Finder or Auto Vocab Builder to study just what's actually new.
